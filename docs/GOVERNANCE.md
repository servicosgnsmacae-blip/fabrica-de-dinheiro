# Governance — AgencyOS v0.1

## 1. Authority model

The Mother Agency governs. Asset Agencies execute within explicit boundaries. Gustavo remains the final owner of the system and final allocator of capital.

## 2. Governance domains

Governance covers:

- permissions;
- autonomy levels;
- budget limits;
- publication authority;
- policy enforcement;
- risk thresholds;
- quality gates;
- source requirements;
- escalation rules;
- change management;
- recovery and retirement authority.

## 3. Separation of duties

Creation and approval must be separated for material outputs. The same agent may not be the sole creator and final approver of its work.

## 4. Least privilege

Every agent, tool and integration receives only the permissions required for its role. Read, write, publish, spend and policy-changing capabilities must be separable.

## 5. Autonomy levels

- **L0 — Manual:** all meaningful actions require human execution or approval.
- **L1 — AI Assisted:** AI prepares work; humans execute.
- **L2 — Supervised Automation:** low-risk execution can be automated; important actions require approval.
- **L3 — Autonomous Operations:** routine production runs autonomously; exceptions escalate.
- **L4 — Self-Optimizing:** the agency can propose and run controlled improvements.
- **L5 — Autonomous Portfolio:** the Mother Agency can create, fund, scale, recover and retire agencies within pre-approved limits.

Autonomy is earned, explicit and reversible.

## 6. Change control

Material changes must not move directly to production. They require validation proportional to risk and, where applicable, Champion/Challenger testing.

## 7. Escalation classes

### Critical
Account suspension, security incident, strike, monetization loss, policy breach with major exposure.

### High
Unexpected spending, severe revenue drop, repeated publication failure, major external dependency failure.

### Medium
Performance anomaly, inconclusive experiment, localized quality issue.

### Low
Routine events handled automatically within policy.

## 8. Blast-radius control

Agencies must be isolated by permissions, budget, credentials, memory scope and execution boundaries. A local failure must not become a portfolio failure.

## 9. Governance records

Every important governance decision should reference the applicable policy, evidence, decision maker, scope, date and expected consequence.
