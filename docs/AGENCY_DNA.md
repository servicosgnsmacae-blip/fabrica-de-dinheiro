# Agency DNA — AgencyOS v0.1

## 1. Definition

Agency DNA is the constitutional specification of an Asset Agency. It defines what the agency is, why it exists, how it operates, what it may do and when it should stop.

## 2. Required fields

Every Asset Agency must define at least:

- `agency_id`
- `agency_type`
- `asset_id`
- `platform`
- `market`
- `language`
- `niche`
- `audience`
- `business_model`
- `primary_goal`
- `north_star_metric`
- `brand_identity`
- `content_policy`
- `risk_policy`
- `budget`
- `autonomy_level`
- `tools_allowed`
- `sources_allowed`
- `publishing_rules`
- `learning_policy`
- `scaling_rules`
- `kill_criteria`

## 3. Identity

Identity defines the asset name, market, audience, language, positioning and brand characteristics. It must remain distinct from provider-specific configuration.

## 4. Economic mandate

Every agency must know how it is expected to create economic value. This includes its monetization model, budget limits, expected unit economics, scaling criteria and failure thresholds.

## 5. Editorial and operational policy

The DNA defines permitted themes, prohibited themes, acceptable source classes, content formats, quality requirements, publication constraints and escalation rules.

## 6. Capabilities and permissions

Capabilities describe what the agency can request. Permissions describe what it is actually authorized to execute at its current autonomy level.

## 7. Autonomy

Autonomy is explicit, progressive and reversible. An agency cannot infer additional authority from successful past actions.

## 8. Memory scope

Every agency has its own memory namespace. Reusable knowledge may be promoted to platform-level memory only through governed transfer.

## 9. Scaling rules

Scale requires evidence. Rules should define what economic, operational and risk thresholds must be met before increasing budget or production.

## 10. Kill criteria

Kill criteria must exist at birth. They may include persistent negative economics, unacceptable risk, monetization loss, strategic irrelevance or repeated failure after controlled recovery attempts.

## 11. Versioning

Agency DNA is versioned. Material changes create a new version and must not silently overwrite the governing specification of an active agency.
